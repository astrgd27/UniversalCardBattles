# NIGHTFALL: The City Will Fall (official module)

The first official module: the design by @Astral (*NIGHTFALL Game Design Document v0.1*), built as data in `packages/reference-games/nightfall`. The JSON is generated by `tools/nightfall/build.py`: edit the script, then run `python3 tools/nightfall/build.py && npm run -s validate packages/reference-games/nightfall`.

## What is in
- **The core set**:
  - 5 Masters, 76 faction cards, 12 neutral cards, the 2 set-aside cards and the 8 two-sided Districts. A claimed District turns to its Fallen side.
  - Two tokens: the Shambler and the Mandrake.
  - The 5 starter decks: "Outbreak at Site 9", "The Marsh-Whitcombe Trust", "Don't Go in There", "The Barrow Wakes" and "Nine Moons".
- **The City** is the board. The five Districts sit in the middle row, with each player's units and structures on their own side. Citizens are a counter on each District.
  - Adjacency follows the row, and the Subway is adjacent to every District.
  - The Night Track is the game var `hour` (0 = 8 PM … 9 = 5 AM). The Witching Hour (+1 Menace for Horrors) is hour 4, and the Last Hour (−1 Menace for Vampires) is hour 9.
- **Turn**: Rise, Draw, Gather, Night, Toll.
  - The first player skips their first draw, and the second player starts with 1 extra resource.
  - Hand size is 7 at Toll. The hour advances after the second player's Toll, and Dawn follows the 5 AM round.
- **Hunting**: Prey, Clash, Strike, Raid and Investigate, plus Intercept.
  - A Hunt exhausts the unit, and Relentless units hunt twice.
  - Fresh units can't Hunt unless they are Swift.
- **Response step**: Prey, Raid and every card played open a response step that only the defending player takes part in.
  - In it the defender can Intercept, which the engine offers only when an Intercept is possible.
  - A Cabal defender can also play Fast Schemes, Drain or Mend.
  - Nobody else ever has to pass.
- **Every win condition**: three Districts, Ruin (Essence 0), Body Count 13, the Child surviving to its owner's next Rise, and Dawn. At Dawn the most claims wins, then the most trophies, then the highest Essence.
- **Every faction rule**:
  - **Blood Cabal**: Vitae, Thrall pens (6), Feed, Drain, Thirst and Frenzy, Mend, Domination, Fast Schemes, Old Money.
  - **Harvest Cult**: Bounty, the Wheel of the Year (Sowing, Growing, Reaping, Fallow), the Field and Growth, Yields, Offerings and the Barrow, Effigies, the Old One's Awakening and Harvest.
  - **Zombie Horde**: Biomass, the Morgue, sacrificing Shamblers, Infect and Devour, Spread, Overrun, Site 9 Labs.
  - **Slasher Killers**: Terror, Lurking (face down, at most 2 per District), revealing to Hunt with Ambush +2, Traps, bluffs discarded when Investigated, Slay and Panic, Vanish, Undying and Sequels, Body Count.
  - **Devil's Own**: Sin, Corrupt and Souls, Gestation with a Vessel, Omens and their Portents, the Nativity and the Child, Possession (gain control), Bargains and Pacts.

## Where the digital version interprets the document
These are choices made where the document leaves room, or where a rule needs a physical gesture. All are easy to change in `build.py`.

| Rule | In this version |
|---|---|
| Mulligan once | During setup, each player picks any cards to shuffle back |
| Frenzy "must Hunt this turn if it can" | At the start of Night the Frenzied Vampire Hunts automatically: it Preys if it can, otherwise its owner picks a unit for it to Clash, otherwise it Strikes |
| Fast, Drain and Mend "at any time" | On your own turn, and in response steps (when the opponent plays a card or declares a Hunt) |
| Reveal to Hunt (Lurkers) | Revealing is its own action (pay the cost, flip); Ambush +2 applies to that Killer's Hunts that turn. Dreamwalker moves Mister Lullaby when he reveals |
| Traps | Every Trap springs when Investigated (Bear Trap wounds the investigator); a Trap you can't pay for fizzles |
| The Phone Call "reveals their hand" | The opponent's hand is laid face up, you discard an Event, the rest go back |
| Trophies | Every Citizen a player takes counts once (Thralls, Offerings, Infected/Devoured, Victims, Souls) |
| Hazmat "first wound each turn" | Resets at every Rise |
| Exsanguinate X | X is chosen when it resolves: pay X Vitae, Drain X Thralls (each Drain gives 2 Vitae as usual), deal 2X wounds or X damage |

Also implemented exactly as written:
- the random first player;
- Guard holding a unit back until it has Clashed with a Guard;
- the Revolver's silver wounds, which can't be Mended or prevented;
- the Deep Tunnels once per turn;
- the Final Girl Ending a Killer;
- the advanced District draft. A deck may bring exactly 3 Districts (deck section `brought`). If both players do, one of the 6 is removed at random, and the players place the other 5 alternately, starting with the second player.

## The store (campaign "The Long Night")
- **Start:** you begin with nothing. In the Store you can claim one free promo starter: Outbreak at Site 9, The Marsh-Whitcombe Trust, Don't Go in There or The Barrow Wakes. Claiming one locks the others.
- **Battles:** each starter deck is an AI rival, and every battle is replayable. Each battle pays Marks:
  - first win: 150 and a Blood Dawn booster;
  - later wins: 60;
  - a loss: 20.
- **The Witching Hour rivals:** these unlock after you beat their Dusk version. They start with +5 Essence and +2 resource, and pay:
  - first win: 300 and two boosters;
  - later wins: 100;
  - a loss: 30.
- **Blood Dawn booster** (100 Marks), the core set: 6 commons, 3 uncommons (Districts included), and 1 rare or Legendary. Legendary cards are 1 in 5 of that slot and include the Masters, the Old One and the Child.
- **Collection:** decks for campaign battles must be built from cards you own.

## Tutorials
Six scripted lessons on the module's Tutorials menu, each worth 10 Marks the first time it is completed:
- **Basics:** controls, Districts and Citizens, playing units, Hunting, Intercepts, the Toll and claiming a District.
- **Blood Cabal:** Feed, Influence and Domination, Drain, and a Fast Scheme on the opponent's turn.
- **Harvest Cult:** the Wheel of the Year, Yields, Offerings and the Old One's Harvest.
- **Zombie Horde:** Infect, Spread, sacrifices, Strains and Mutations, and Overrun.
- **Slasher Killers:** Lurking, Traps, revealing, Slay and Panic, Vanish, and the Final Act.
- **Devil's Own:** the Vessel, Omens, Bargains and Pacts, the Nativity and the Child's victory.

Each is a set piece built in `tools/nightfall/build.py` (procedures `tutCity`, `tutHand`, `tutPut`, `tutLurk`, `tutPlant`, `tutOmen`, and the game var `noMulligan`).

## Balance (v1.1)
Tuned with AI-vs-AI playtests, every faction pairing played from both seats. The AI sees only what a player sees.

**Tooling:**
- Run `cp tools/nightfall/playtest.ts packages/test-harness/tests/_pt.ts && node packages/test-harness/tests/_pt.ts --games=4`. It prints:
  - how games end;
  - win rate by faction and per pairing;
  - unspent resource and hand size;
  - faction counters and actions per game.
- `--log=deckA,deckB,seed` prints one game turn by turn.
- Every number below is a lever in `LEVERS` at the top of `tools/nightfall/build.py`.
  - `NF_<LEVER>=n` overrides one for an experiment.
  - `NF_OUT=<dir>` builds the variant elsewhere, so several can run in parallel.
- `python3 tools/nightfall/gdd_update.py` rewrites the design document from the current levers. It reads the original `docs/NIGHTFALL_GDD_v0.1.docx` and writes `docs/NIGHTFALL_GDD.docx`.

**Result:** win rate across all pairings went from Zombie 100% / Cabal 88% / Harvest 44% / Slasher 38% / Devil 6% to Zombie 53% / Cabal 63% / Harvest 50% / Slasher 38% / Devil 47% (80 games).
- Every signature win now happens. In 80 games: 14 Prophecies, 10 Old One awakenings, 32 Dominations, 29 Overruns and 2 Body Count wins.
- The Slashers are the faction to watch: Body Count averages about 5 of 11.
- Games end by Dawn about half the time, by the City about a fifth of the time, and otherwise by Ruin or a signature win.

| Change | v0.1 (document) | v1.1 | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strikes | full Menace, any number | one per District per turn, at most 2 damage | Strikes decided two games in three; about 17 of 18 Citizens were still on the board at the end |
| Shamblers | Prey (Devour) and Strike | Mindless: no Prey, no Strike (Feeding Frenzy still Devours) | every Infect made a free Shambler that Preyed next turn: an exponential snowball |
| Spread | every District with 3+ Zombies | one District of your choice | the same snowball |
| Zombie Gather | 2 | 0 (the Morgue, Devours and sacrifices only) | about 14 Biomass unspent per game; still about 65% with mindless Shamblers |
| Sacrifice | a death | a payment | bug: sacrifice + Morgue + Patient Zero paid 3 Biomass per token |
| Domination | Influence ≥ Citizens | also needs your Vampire there (the document's own lever) | Cabal won 84% by Dominating with Puppets and Influence |
| Evacuation Order | move 2 Citizens | at least 1 stays behind | Prey + Evacuation claimed a District on turn 1 |
| Slasher Gather / Draw | 1 / 1 | 3 / 2 | 1.3 cards in hand at the end of the game; Lurking eats a card a turn |
| Body Count | 13 Victims wins | no instant win: the **Final Act** at 11 Victims (below) | it averaged 5 at the end of the game; a power spike plays better than a counter that ends the game |
| Harvest Gather | 1 | 3 | 6–8 unplayable cards with no Bounty |
| Awaken | 10 | 6 | the Old One woke in 1 game in 40 |
| Devil Gather / Draw | 1 / 1 | 2 / 2 | too few cards to find Omens and the Vessel |
| Gestation | starts at 0, Omens only | still 0 to 9, plus 3 at each Rise with a Vessel | the Nativity almost never happened |
| Augustin's Dark Gift | 3 Sin | 2 Sin | the Vessel search was too dear to use early |

**The Final Act (Slashers):**
- When the Body Count reaches 11, the Final Act begins (a banner announces it). It lasts for the rest of the game. Killers get +2 Menace and +2 Endurance, Strike for their full Menace (other units deal at most 2), and can't be Ended.
- A destroyed Killer returns at the Slasher's next Rise, and each face-up Killer Slays a Citizen in its District at the Slasher's Toll.
- Strikes against Old Hollow deal at most 1. The Final Act doesn't win by itself. The text is on Old Hollow's card, and the levers are `FINAL_ACT` and `FA_BUFF`.
- `BODYCOUNT_WIN > 0` restores the old instant win.

**AI fix found along the way:**
- When the AI weighed a move against passing, the pass line ran on into Strikes and the opponent's Rise, while every other line was scored before them. Any neutral move therefore looked better than passing: the Slasher AI shuffled its Lurkers about 28 times a game.
- The new `ai.horizon: "pass"` scores each move after the agent also passes (see `docs/DSL.md`).

## Art
- Drop images in with `python3 tools/art/import.py nightfall <folder>`. Images are matched by collector number (`NF-012`), card id or card name.
- Add `--full` for finished full-card images; without it, images are used as picture-window art.
- Name files `back`, `Blood Dawn`/`pack-blood-dawn` or `deck-<deck id>` for the card back, the booster and the starter-deck boxes.
